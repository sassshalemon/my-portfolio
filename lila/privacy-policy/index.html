Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 August 2026

Lila is developed and published by Mimori Studio. Lila is designed as a local-first app for remembering everyday information, people, belongings, routines and reminders.

1. Information you enter in Lila

Lila lets you choose to enter information such as personal details, details about people you know, information about your home or belongings, notes, dates, repeat rules and reminder information.

This information is stored locally on your device. Lila does not send this content to Mimori Studio or to third-party servers.

2. Data collection and sharing

Lila does not collect or share user data off your device. The release version of Lila does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, cloud accounts or other network services to transmit your Lila content.

3. Internet access

Lila is designed to work offline. The release app does not request the Android INTERNET permission.

4. Local reminders and notifications

If you choose to use reminders, Lila may request permission to show notifications. Reminder scheduling is handled locally on your device. Reminder content is not transmitted to Mimori Studio.

5. Storage and security

Lila stores its app data in Android app-private storage on your device. The release configuration disables Android application backup for Lila. Because Lila does not transmit your Lila data to a server, there is no server-side copy maintained by Mimori Studio.

6. Retention and deletion

Your Lila data remains on your device until you delete it using the app, clear Lila's app storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Since Mimori Studio does not receive or store your Lila content on a server, there is no remote user-data account to delete.

7. Accounts

Lila does not require or provide a user account.

8. Third parties

Lila does not sell user data and does not share your Lila content with advertisers, analytics providers or other third parties.

9. Children's privacy

Lila is a general-audience utility app and is not specifically directed at children. Lila does not knowingly collect personal information from users because app content is not transmitted to Mimori Studio.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

If Lila's data practices change in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those changes are released, as required.
